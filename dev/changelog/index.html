Changelog

All notable changes to SchematicSVG.jl. The format follows Keep a Changelog, and versions follow Semantic Versioning (before 1.0, minor versions may change behaviour).

Unreleased

Changes since v0.7.2.

Added

  • layout combines drawings (ASCII strings or Schematics) in a row or a column. Parts join at their terminals with a drawn wire (connect) or by name (share); element and node names used in more than one part get the part number, and supplies and ground stay common. [s1 s2] and [s1; s2] lay out Schematics.
  • Voltage marks: +, − and a label across a component or an open circuit. ASCII O elements (Ovgs) reach across blank cells to the terminals or wires at each end; CircuiTikZ v=, v^=, v<= and to[open, v=…]; the Voltage primitive.
  • Current arrows: ASCII ~i1>, <~i1, ~i1^, ~i1v inside a wire; CircuiTikZ i= and its variants (i_=, i<=, i>_=, …) placed as CircuiTikZ places them; the Current primitive.
  • layout names joined nodes after working out every join, so chained joins keep plain names (joining b–b and then c–b gives nodes b and c, not b_1).
  • Supply rails and compacting in layout: in a row, the parts' VDD (and other top supplies) line up at the top of the figure and VSS at the bottom, by lengthening the vertical wires that lead to them. In a column, each stage's supplies line up on its own top and bottom lines, and stages stack gap apart from one stage's VSS line to the next stage's VDD line; a supply with a clear way up (or down) beside the other stages goes on to the figure's top (or bottom) rail. Long runs of vertical wire are shortened to one row, before placing and again in the finished figure. rails=false and compact=false turn these off.
  • Node labels: label_nodes(s), or nodelabels=true in ascii_schematic and tikz_schematic, labels every node that doesn't already show a name with its netlist name (n1, n2, …); nodes=:all / nodelabels=:all also labels ground and supplies. layout(…; nodelabels=true) labels the combined circuit, including joined nodes.
  • Sinusoidal sources in ASCII drawings: SVsig is a sinusoidal voltage source named Vsig and SIb a sinusoidal current source named Ib, with the same direction markers as V and I. Give them (amplitude=…, frequency=…, offset=…) or SPICE text such as "SIN(0 10m 1k)"; the annotation shows "10 mV, 1 kHz".
  • Captions: ASCII "text", <"text" and "text">; the Caption primitive.
  • xscale / yscale spread or tighten a drawing without resizing symbols or text, as keywords of ascii_schematic, tikz_schematic and Schematic, and from a CircuiTikZ environment's own xscale=/yscale=. Schematic(s; kw...) copies a drawing with new settings.
  • Border: a thick gray frame with rounded corners around every figure, so a drawing that runs past the edge of a notebook window is easy to spot (border=false turns it off).
  • Inferred subcircuit terminals: to_subcircuit and spice_subcircuit use the drawn terminals (named terminal circles, left to right) when terminals isn't given. SchematicNetlist has a new terminals field, and :terminals in external stands for them.
  • Documentation site (Documenter.jl), this changelog, and GitHub Actions for tests and documentation.

Changed

  • When xscale/yscale squeeze a drawing, transistor and op-amp leads shorten without running into the symbol: a PMOS gate wire stops outside the bubble, and an op-amp keeps its inputs in place and slides right. Two-terminal symbols (resistors, capacitors, sources, …) whose ends are closer than the symbol is long, because of a small scale, xscale or yscale, shrink to fit with a short lead at each end.
  • The examples notebook's amplifier subcircuits use inferred terminals (except the differential pair, which keeps its explicit order), and it adds a two-stage CS + SF amplifier built with layout.

Fixed

  • SPICE text no longer contains Unitful's unit strings (such as 4.7 kΩ), which SPICE can't read: quantities are written as plain numbers, by Microelectronics' qstr when it is loaded and otherwise by a small Unitful extension.
  • On Julia 1.13, some values gained rounding noise in SPICE text (1e-12 became 1.0000000000000002e-12); numbers are now written in their shortest form.
  • Tests that used the unexported Opamp now use SchematicSVG.Opamp.
  • Loading the Microelectronics extension could fail with $UndefVarError: `SchematicSVGMicroelectronicsExt` not defined in `Main`$, depending on how it was loaded; the extension modules no longer carry docstrings.
