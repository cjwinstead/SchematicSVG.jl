Placed primitives

A Schematic is a list of placed primitives, and you can build one directly. Coordinates are in CircuiTikZ units with y pointing up.

using SchematicSVG

Schematic([
    Bipole(:V, Pt(0, 1), Pt(0, 0), raw"$v_{in}$"), Ground(Pt(0, 0)),
    Wire(Pt(0, 1), PinRef("Q1", :B)),
    Transistor(:npn, "Q1", Pt(1, 1)),
    Bipole(:R, Pt(1, 2.5), Pt(1, 1.5), raw"$R_C$"), Wire(Pt(1, 1.5), PinRef("Q1", :C)),
    Supply(Pt(1, 2.5), "V_{CC}", true),
    Wire(PinRef("Q1", :E), Pt(1, 0)), Ground(Pt(1, 0)),
])
PrimitiveNotes
Bipole(kind, a, b, label; name, note, side)kind ∈ :R :C :L :D :V :I :sV :cV :cI :box :open :short; drawn along a→b. For sources a is SPICE n+ (+ near a; current arrow a→b). side = :auto, or :left/:right of a→b like CircuiTikZ.
Transistor(kind, name, at; xflip, yflip, label, note, w, h):npn :pnp :nmos :pmos; pins :B/:G, :C/:D, :E/:S via pin or PinRef, at w (control) and h (top/bottom) from the centre
SchematicSVG.Opamp(name, at; label, note, w, h, inv_up)pins :inp, :inn, :out; inputs at (−w, ±h). Not exported, so it doesn't clash with Microelectronics' Opamp.
Wire(points...)points are Pt or PinRef(device, pin)
Port(at, label, side; name), NodeLabel(at, name, side; label)terminal with circle / node name only; side ∈ :left :right :above :below
Ground(at, flip=false), Supply(at, label, up), Dot(at)
Voltage(plus, minus, label; side, kind)polarity marks: on the line for kind=:open, flanking the symbol of a component of that kind
Current(a, b, label; side, into, pos, kind)current arrow on the wire a→b, pointing at b (at a when into), pos of the way along
Caption(at, text; align)free text centred on at (align=:left/:right to start/end there)

note is the second line of text (value or model) that annotations fill in.

Currents at a node

To mark the currents branching at a node, give each branch its own arrow starting at the node; into=true marks a current entering it.

node = Pt(0, 0)
Schematic([
    Wire(Pt(-1.5, 0), Pt(1.5, 0)), Wire(node, Pt(0, -1.5)), Dot(node),
    Current(node, Pt(-1.5, 0), "i_1"; into=true, pos=0.4),
    Current(node, Pt(1.5, 0), "i_2"; pos=0.4),
    Current(node, Pt(0, -1.5), "i_3"; pos=0.4),
])

Labels

Labels accept a small subset of LaTeX: $…$ is stripped, _x / _{xy} become subscripts (\_ is a literal underscore), \mathrm{…}/\text{…} keep their text, and Greek letters plus \infty \cdot \times \pm \approx \parallel \, \; map to Unicode.

Settings

Schematic(items; scale=50, stroke="#222", background="white", font="serif", xscale=1, yscale=1, border=true) sets pixels per unit, line colour, background ("none" for transparent), label font, the layout spread (Spacing and layout) and the border around the figure. Schematic(s; kw...) copies a drawing with new settings, and to_svg(s) returns the SVG text to save or embed elsewhere.
